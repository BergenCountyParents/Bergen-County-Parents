Summer movie nights are one of the easiest free outings in the county, and nearly every town runs its own series at some point between June and September.

Englewood's series runs on the second Friday of the month in Mackay Park, with a family-friendly pick each time and food trucks setting up an hour before sundown. Bring a blanket and get there early for the good spots on the lawn.

Several neighboring towns run similar series on different weeks, which means with a little planning you can catch one almost every Friday through the end of summer without repeating a town twice.
