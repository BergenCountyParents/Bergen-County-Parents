Rainy Saturdays don't have to mean another afternoon of screen time. Bergen County has more indoor options than most parents realize, once you get past the usual trampoline park.

The Fair Lawn library's children's wing has a dedicated play area that rarely gets crowded, even on the rainiest days — worth checking their events calendar too, since they run free drop-in activities most weekends.

Beyond that, a handful of climbing gyms and a couple of smaller children's museums round out the list. None of them require advance booking, which matters when the rain wasn't in the forecast until that morning.
