About Bergen County Parents

A local resource for families across Bergen County — things to do, what's happening, and the practical stuff that doesn't come with a welcome packet.

Photo placeholder — county scenery or team photo

Bergen County Parents started with a simple frustration: finding out what's actually worth doing with your kids in this county means digging through a dozen town Facebook groups, three different Instagram accounts, and whatever your neighbor happens to mention at pickup. We wanted one place that pulled it together.

We're not a syndicated national site running local keywords for traffic. Everything here is written by people who actually live in Bergen County, dealing with the same kindergarten cutoffs and splash pad closures as everyone reading it. Where we can, we credit contributors by name — parents from Ridgewood, Tenafly, Hackensack, and everywhere in between who know their corner of the county better than we ever could from a desk.

Actually current

We update posts when things change — closures, new dates, corrected details — instead of leaving stale info up indefinitely.

Written locally

No syndicated content, no national franchise. Every post is written by someone who lives here.

No sponsored filler

If something's listed, it's because it's genuinely worth your Saturday — not because someone paid for the placement.

Want to write for us? We're always open to guest contributors covering their own town — reach out through the contact page and tell us what you'd want to write about.
